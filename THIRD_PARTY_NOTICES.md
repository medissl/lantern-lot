# Lantern Lot asset provenance

The new overhaul raster and audio files under `assets/pixel/` and `assets/audio/`
are original work authored for Lantern Lot on 14–15 October 2026 by the
implementation team using Codex-assisted coordinate drawing and mathematical
waveform synthesis. No previous rejected overhaul asset was reused. No
reference image, online texture, sprite, sound recording, font or other external
component was incorporated into these files.

These original asset files and their editable sources `art/build_pixel_art.py`
and `art/build_inspect_ui.py` carry a **CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)** dedication
to the extent copyright applies to the original contributions. The dedication
and fallback license permit commercial use, modification and redistribution
without attribution. This is the project's stated asset-use basis, not a
certification that machine-assisted output is copyrightable in every territory.
The license text is
available at <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode>.
This dedication applies to these new asset files and their drawing source,
not to the rest of the game, Godot, or separately licensed dependencies.

Each asset's dimensions, pivot, animation indices, origin, source, review status,
creator, commercial-use basis and license are recorded in
`assets/visual_manifest.json`. The original unavailable museum reference was
not copied, shipped, or inspected. Visual direction follows the written brief;
exact matching of the unavailable reference cannot be established.
